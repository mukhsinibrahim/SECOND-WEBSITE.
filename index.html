Our Projects

Kairon

FIRST PROJECT

100% completed.

Kairon is an AI-driven smart scheduling application engineered to eliminate the manual hassle of managing college & schools timetables.
The project leverages AI-powered OCR to automatically extract complex course schedules directly from uploaded syllabus PDFs and images. By integrating a natural language AI assistant, Kairon allows students to interactively chat with their calendar for real-time updates, demonstrating a practical application of automation, high-performance security, and user-centric design.

Notiva

SECOND PROJECT

88% completion.

Notiva acts as a powerful, full-stack central nerve center that instantly organizes the chaotic student experience. Developed with an advanced JavaScript and TypeScript architecture, the platform pairs a robust administrative API backend with a visually stunning, responsive client-side interface. Instead of forcing students to log into multiple disconnected, confusing college portals, Notiva consolidates daily class schedules, lecture notes, study repositories, and pending homework assignments into a single, intuitive visual command center. By tracking deadlines in real time and providing clear analytics on pending tasks, it creates an immersive, high-efficiency academic ecosystem that transforms how students master their daily routines.

Simulation Lab

THIRD PROJECT

75% completion.

ATC SimLab is an AI-augmented educational simulation platform engineered to transform abstract, complex engineering and computer science concepts into immersive, hands-on learning experiences
Developed to bridge the gap left by static text textbooks and limited physical laboratory hardware, the platform provides interactive web-based simulations where students can drag, drop, and step through real-time procedures—such as watching an algorithm sort data or tracking packet paths in a network topology.
Powered by a robust FastAPI and React architecture, the application integrates Mistral AI to act as a 24/7 personal tutor. It automatically watches the student's actions to provide context-aware hints, generate adaptive quizzes that adjust to their learning level, and instantly break down dense syllabus paragraphs into plain language notes. By turning intimidating chalkboards into interactive digital games, ATC SimLab empowers students to truly master practical skills with zero manual burden on professors.

Tamisemi Sim Lab

FOURTH PROJECT

1% completion.

TAMISEMI Simulation Lab is a planned interactive learning and digital science laboratory platform designed specifically for Tanzanian secondary school education (Form 1 to Form 4). Mirroring the core architecture of ATC SimLab, the project aims to turn abstract syllabus concepts into practical, step-by-step web-based simulations. By providing a virtual environment to safely conduct science experiments and technical practicals, the platform bridges the gap of limited physical school laboratory hardware and enhances conceptual understanding for secondary school students.
